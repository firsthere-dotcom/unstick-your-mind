# Unstick Your Mind

A landing page for a creative and cathartic project that turns stuck thoughts and feelings into personal songs.

## What it is

Participants answer guided questions about a thought, memory, or feeling that keeps returning. Their words are shaped into song lyrics, and Suno turns those lyrics into a finished song — theirs to keep.

This is a non-commercial project by Klara and Kata.

## How it works

1. **Reflect** — answer questions about the stuck thought or feeling
2. **Map** — trace its shape, what it holds, what it connects to
3. **Write** — words become honest lyrics drawn from what was shared
4. **Compose** — Suno sets the lyrics to music
5. **Listen** — the finished song is yours to keep and return to
